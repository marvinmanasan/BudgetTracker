# Bulsa 1.0.0

- Default and custom wallets with starting balances, renaming, inclusion and hiding/restoring.
- Total Budget combining General and included wallets.
- Expense and income recording with wallet assignment, categories, dates and optional details.
- Custom expense categories and income sources.
- Transaction editing, reassignment, deletion and Undo.
- Activity history, record details, search and filters.
- Recorded transfers between wallets.
- Wallet balance adjustments and Total Budget corrections.
- Monthly spending limits, previous-month limit copying and limit history.
- Category limits, Everything Else and spending progress.
- A savings goal with contributions, history and progress.
- Local display name and Light, Dark or System appearance.
- Wallet-first introduction and selective financial-record reset.
- On-device storage and peso amount formatting.
