# Using Bulsa

Bulsa helps you record money manually, manage wallets and plan spending. Dates use MM-DD-YYYY. Month fields use MM-YYYY.

## Quick start

1. On Welcome to Bulsa, tap **Set Up Wallets**.
2. Open a wallet such as Cash. Enter **Starting Balance (PHP)** and tap **Save Starting Balance**.
3. For your own wallet, open **Wallets**, tap **Add Wallet**, enter its name and starting balance, then tap **Add Wallet**.
4. Return to **Overview** and tap **+ Add Expense**.
5. Enter **Amount (PHP)**, choose a **Category**, select a wallet using the **Wallet:** button, and check **Date (MM-DD-YYYY)**.
6. Tap **Save Expense**. The record appears in **Activity**.

## Add a wallet

1. On **Overview**, tap **View Wallets ›**, then **Add Wallet**.
2. Enter **Wallet Name** and **Starting Balance (PHP)**.
3. Choose whether **Include in Total Budget** is on.
4. Tap **Add Wallet**.

The wallet appears in Wallets. Starting balance is not income.

## Set a starting balance

1. In **Wallets**, open a wallet showing **Starting Balance Not Set**.
2. Enter **Starting Balance (PHP)**.
3. Tap **Save Starting Balance**.

Use the balance before the transactions you will record. Zero is allowed. After setup, use Adjust Balance for balance corrections.

## Include or exclude a wallet

1. Open **Wallets**.
2. Turn **Include in Total Budget** on or off beneath a wallet.

Only whether its balance counts in Total Budget changes. Its records remain saved, and no transaction is created.

## Add an expense

1. On **Overview**, tap **+ Add Expense**.
2. Enter **Amount (PHP)** and choose a **Category**.
3. Tap the **Wallet:** button and choose the wallet used.
4. Check **Date (MM-DD-YYYY)**.
5. To add a payment-method label or note, tap **More Details · Optional**.
6. Tap **Save Expense**.

Activity shows the expense. The wallet balance falls. Total Budget falls if that wallet is included.

## Add income

1. Tap **+ Add Expense**, then choose **Income**.
2. Enter **Amount (PHP)** and choose an **Income Source**.
3. Choose the receiving wallet and check the date.
4. Tap **Save Income**.

The wallet balance rises. Total Budget rises if the wallet is included.

## Choose or create a category

1. In the transaction form, tap a choice under **Category** or **Income Source**.
2. To create one, tap **Add Custom**.
3. Enter **Custom Name**, then tap **Save Choice**.
4. Complete and save the transaction.

The custom choice becomes selected. Expense categories and income sources use separate lists.

## Manage custom choices

1. In the transaction form, tap **Manage Custom**.
2. To rename, tap **Rename**, enter **Rename Choice**, then tap **Save Choice**.
3. To remove a choice, tap **Remove**, then confirm with **Remove**.
4. Tap **Done Managing**.

Old transactions keep their original names. Preset choices are retained.

## Use Not Specified and General

1. In a transaction form, tap the **Wallet:** button.
2. On **Choose Wallet**, tap **Not Specified**.
3. Save the transaction.
4. Open **Wallets** to see **General · Always Included**.

Income adds to General and expenses subtract from it. General always counts in Total Budget.

## Record a transfer

1. In **Wallets**, tap **Transfer Between Wallets**.
2. Tap **From: Choose Wallet** and choose the source.
3. Tap **To: Choose Wallet** and choose the destination.
4. Enter **Amount (PHP)**, check the date and add an optional note.
5. Tap **Review Transfer**, check the balances, then tap **Confirm Transfer**.

Activity shows Transfer. Both wallets must be visible and have starting balances. The source needs enough recorded money. Bulsa records the transfer; it does not send real money.

## Adjust a balance

1. Open the wallet and tap **Adjust Balance**.
2. Enter **Actual Balance (PHP)** and a **Reason**.
3. Tap **Review Adjustment**.
4. Check the difference, then tap **Confirm Adjustment**.

The balance is corrected. Activity shows Balance Adjustment. An unchanged balance creates no adjustment.

## Correct Total Budget

1. On **Overview**, tap **Edit Total Budget**.
2. Enter **New Total Budget (PHP)**.
3. Under **Wallet Receiving the Difference**, choose an included wallet with a starting balance.
4. Enter a **Reason** and tap **Review Change**.
5. Check the result and tap **Confirm Adjustment**.

Only the chosen wallet receives the difference. General, other wallets and spending limits stay unchanged.

## Edit a transaction

1. In **Activity**, tap an expense or income.
2. On **Record Details**, tap **Edit Transaction**.
3. Correct the fields and tap **Review Changes**.
4. Check the balances, then tap **Save Changes**.

The original record is updated. Income and spending follow its corrected amount and date.

## Assign a wallet to an old transaction

1. Open the transaction in **Activity** and tap **Edit Transaction**.
2. Tap the **Wallet:** button and select the correct wallet.
3. Tap **Review Changes** and check the old and new balance effects.
4. Tap **Save Changes**.

The old money effect is reversed and applied to the chosen wallet. For a record previously using Not Specified, its effect is removed from General.

## Delete a transaction

1. Open the expense or income from **Activity**.
2. Tap **Delete Transaction**.
3. Review the balances and tap **Confirm Delete**.

The record is removed. Choose **Keep Record** instead to cancel.

## Undo a deletion

1. In **Activity**, find the deletion notice.
2. Tap **Undo**.

The original transaction returns. Undo remains available until dismissed or the app process closes. Resolve one deletion before deleting another.

## Rename a wallet

1. Open the wallet and tap **Rename Wallet**.
2. Change **Wallet Name**.
3. Tap **Save Name**.

The wallet keeps its balance and history.

## Hide or restore a wallet

1. Open the wallet and tap **Hide Wallet**.
2. To restore it, open **Wallets**, then **Manage Hidden Wallets**.
3. Open the hidden wallet and tap **Restore Wallet**.

Hiding removes it from new transaction choices. An included hidden wallet still counts in Total Budget.

## Delete an unused wallet

1. Open the wallet and tap **More Wallet Options**.
2. Tap **Delete Unused Wallet** and confirm with **Delete**.

Only wallets with no financial history can be deleted. A zero starting balance still counts as history. Use Hide Wallet for a used wallet.

## Use Activity

1. Open **Activity** and enter text in **Search Activity**.
2. Tap **Filters** to choose **Month (MM-YYYY)**, **Record Type**, **Category or Source**, or **Payment Method**.
3. Tap a result for its details.
4. Tap **Clear All Filters** to remove the search and filters.

Records are grouped by date, newest dates first. The spending card shows all expenses for its month, before the other Activity filters.

## View one wallet's Activity

1. Open a wallet and tap **View Wallet Activity**.
2. Read or search its records.
3. Tap **Show All Activity** to remove the wallet restriction.

The wallet view includes its transactions, transfers and balance changes.

## Set a monthly spending limit

1. In **Budgets**, enter **Month (MM-YYYY)**.
2. Tap **Set Monthly Limit** or **Edit Monthly Limit**.
3. Enter **Spending Limit (PHP)**.
4. Check **Remaining With This Limit** and tap **Save Monthly Limit**.

The limit is a spending plan. It does not add money. Zero is allowed.

## Set category limits

1. In **Budgets**, choose a month with a saved monthly limit.
2. Tap **Edit Category Limits**.
3. Under **Choose Category**, select a category and enter **Category Limit (PHP)**.
4. Tap **Add or Update Allocation**. Repeat for other categories.
5. Tap **Save Category Limits**.

Everything Else receives the unallocated allowance. Use Edit Allocation or Remove Allocation to change rows. Add or clear current input before saving. Named allocations cannot exceed the monthly limit.

## View monthly-limit history

1. In **Budgets**, tap **Monthly Limit History**.
2. Read the saved months and limits.
3. Tap a month's **Edit** button to change it, or **Refresh History** to reload.

When the current month has no limit, Bulsa can copy the immediately previous month's limit. Existing values, including zero, remain. Category allocations and unused allowance are not copied.

## Set or edit a savings goal

1. On **Overview** or **Budgets**, tap **Set Savings Goal**.
2. Enter **Goal Name** and **Savings Target (PHP)**.
3. Tap **Save Savings Goal**.
4. To change it later, open **View Savings**, then tap **Edit Savings Goal**.

Editing preserves previous savings records.

## Record savings

1. Open **View Savings** and tap **Record Savings**.
2. Enter **Amount Saved (PHP)** and check **Date (MM-DD-YYYY)**.
3. Add **Note (Optional)** if wanted.
4. Tap **Save Savings Record**.

Savings History and progress increase. Wallet balances and Total Budget stay unchanged.

## Change name or appearance

1. On **Overview**, tap the profile circle.
2. In **Settings**, enter **Display Name (Optional)** if wanted.
3. Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System** under **Appearance**.
4. Tap **Save Settings**.

Your saved name appears in the greeting. System follows the phone's appearance. Text size follows the phone's text-size setting.

## Reset selected records

1. In **Activity**, tap **Reset Selected Data**.
2. Choose **One Wallet**, **General** or **All Wallets**, and select the wallet if required.
3. Choose the record kinds to remove.
4. Tap **Preview Selection** and check the counts and balance effects.
5. Tap **Continue to Confirmation**.
6. Enter RESET in **Type RESET to Confirm**, then tap **Delete Selected Records**.

This permanently removes the selection. Removing a transfer reverses both wallet effects. Reset Starting Balances is available when all four record kinds are selected. Wallet names, inclusion, hidden status, custom choices, spending plans, savings and settings remain.

## Key ideas

- **Total Budget:** General plus included wallet balances.
- **General:** income and expenses saved with Not Specified. It always counts.
- **Include in Total Budget:** controls whether a wallet balance contributes to the total.
- **Monthly Spending Limit:** a plan for spending, separate from recorded money.
- **Negative balance:** recorded spending or corrections exceed recorded funding.
- **Transfer:** records movement between wallets, rather than earning or spending.
- **Adjustment:** corrects recorded money rather than recording ordinary income or purchases.
- **Savings record:** tracks savings separately from wallet money.

For example, with General at zero: GCash 1,000 and Cash 500, both included, make Total Budget 1,500. Spend 100 from GCash and it becomes 900, with Total Budget 1,400. Transfer 200 from GCash to Cash and they become 700 each. Total Budget stays 1,400.

## Tips

1. Set starting balances before recording the transactions they fund.
2. Choose the wallet explicitly. Payment Method is only a label.
3. Enter amounts such as 1000.50, without commas or a peso sign.
4. Check dates. A future-dated record affects the recorded balance immediately.
5. Check inclusion when a wallet change does not change Total Budget.
6. Edit a mistaken transaction; use Adjust Balance to reconcile a balance while retaining valid transactions.
7. Check savings amounts and dates before saving.
8. Read review screens before confirming changes.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why did Total Budget not change?** An excluded wallet does not contribute to it. Transfers between included wallets preserve it. Savings and limit changes also leave it unchanged.

**Why is General negative?** Expenses without a wallet exceed income recorded there. Reassign a transaction if it belongs to a wallet.

**Why does a hidden wallet count?** Hiding controls visibility. Include in Total Budget controls its contribution.

**Why does spending include excluded wallets?** Monthly spending tracks every expense, independently of wallet inclusion.

**Can I recover a deleted transaction?** Use Undo while the deletion notice is available. Bulk reset does not have Undo.

**Why can I not transfer?** Choose two different visible wallets with starting balances. The source needs enough recorded funds.

**Why do old category names remain?** Records retain the names saved with them. Choice renaming does not rewrite history.

**Does Record Savings move money?** No. It updates savings progress separately.

**What does a zero limit mean?** The saved allowance is zero. It is different from having no limit.

**Does next month copy the old plan?** An absent current-month limit can copy the immediately previous month's limit. Category allocations and unused allowance do not carry over.
